# Router template

## Requirements

- Route an internal 65 mm diameter hole with a 16 mm outside diameter guide bushing and a 6 mm bit.
- The guide wall diameter is finished hole diameter + bushing outside diameter - bit diameter: 75 mm nominal.
- Round plate: 200 mm outside diameter, 6 mm thick, with 0.5 mm chamfers on all four circular edges.
- 3D print flat. Follow the `3d-printing` and `tolerances` skills from the shared SOMA library.
- (assumed) FDM printing and a bed at least 200 × 200 mm; confirm the printer's usable area.
- (assumed) The bushing is concentric with the bit and follows the vertical guide wall. Measure the printed opening and check a test cut before use.
- The nominal guide wall has no extra clearance: deviations change the routed diameter. Adjust for the actual printer and tooling after a test cut.
