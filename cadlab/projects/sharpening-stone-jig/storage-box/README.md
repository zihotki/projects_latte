# Sharpening kit storage box

Open this folder in SOMA with **Open folder**. It is part of the sharpening
stone jig project and has its own model and parameters.

Four 170 × 75 × 6 mm stones stand in separate, ventilated slots. Each slot
is 171 mm long between its end stops and defaults to 7 mm wide, with 0.5 mm
clearance on each side. Adjust stone thickness and slot clearance in SOMA.
Raised rests bring the stones and leather level with the holder at a nominal
97.4 mm above the box underside. Small stops under the lid leave a nominal
0.5 mm gap above their top edges to limit movement. The gap and fit need a
physical test. Three raised rests support each stone above the floor. Hexagonal
holes in the floor and lid and diamond-shaped holes in the dividers and side wall allow air
through the stone section. The outer wall is continuous. The stone dividers
are lower than the stones. Solid ends fill the space between the stops and the
end walls. The side-wall holes continue above the dividers. The external box,
lid, and divider top edges have 0.5 mm chamfers.

A 35 mm bay holds the 190 × 95 × 30 mm jig on its long edge, with up to
3 mm of adhesive feet on its underside and 2 mm total clearance. Change
**Holder feet thickness** (0–4 mm) in SOMA to adjust this bay. An 8 mm bay
holds the 200 × 75 × 5 mm leather. A shared 74.4 mm bay holds the 120 × 35 ×
35 mm compound, the angle guides, and small items including the brush. The fit
of the brush depends on its actual size. The bays do not fully isolate splashes.

The lid seats on the rim and is a loose slip-on cover with no latch or seal. The floor is flat for
adhesive feet; position the feet clear of the vent holes and leave space under
the box for air. Wipe excess water off the stones before storage. Drying time
and physical fit have not been measured; if moisture remains, leave the lid
open. Keep the box upright to retain its contents.

Set **Layout** to **Print** to position the lid top-down. Print the box and
lid separately on the Bambu A1; each part fits the nominal 256 mm bed. Check
Bambu Studio's preview, bed adhesion, and support needs before printing. Print
and test the fit before relying on the lid. Use fine mesh quality when exporting.
The box and lid have not been physically printed or tested.
